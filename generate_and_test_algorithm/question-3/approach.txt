APPROACH

The goal is to find a number between 1 and 50 whose square is 625.

Instead of testing every number from 1 to 50, the search space can be reduced
using the properties of the target.

The target value 625 ends in 25.

A square ending in 25 must come from a number ending in 5.

Therefore, the possible candidates between 1 and 50 are:

5, 15, 25, 35, 45

Generate these candidates one by one and test their squares.

The test is:

candidate × candidate == 625

Testing proceeds as:

5²  = 25    → reject
15² = 225   → reject
25² = 625   → accept and stop

The candidate 25 is therefore the solution.
