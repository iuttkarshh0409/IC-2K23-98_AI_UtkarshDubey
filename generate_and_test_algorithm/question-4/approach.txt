APPROACH

The goal is to find a number between 1 and 100 whose cube is 729.

Two candidate-generation strategies can be considered.

APPROACH 1: Optimized Generation

A number ending in 9 has a cube that also ends in 9.

Since the target 729 ends in 9, numbers ending in 9 are promising candidates.

The candidates between 1 and 100 are:

9, 19, 29, 39, 49, 59, 69, 79, 89, 99

Test each candidate by calculating its cube.

The first candidate is:

9³ = 729

Therefore, the solution is found immediately and the search stops.

APPROACH 2: Traditional Generate-and-Test

A simpler approach is to generate every number sequentially from 1 to 100.

For each candidate, calculate its cube and compare it with 729.

The candidates are tested as:

1³ = 1      → reject
2³ = 8      → reject
3³ = 27     → reject
...
9³ = 729    → accept and stop

The optimized approach requires fewer tests, while the traditional approach
follows the basic Generate-and-Test strategy directly.
