APPROACH

The goal is to find a number between 1 and 20 whose square is 144.

A basic Generate-and-Test approach would generate every number from 1 to 20
and test whether its square is equal to 144.

However, the search space can be reduced using the properties of the target.

Since 144 ends in 4, the candidate number can end in either 2 or 8 because
numbers ending in 2 or 8 produce squares ending in 4.

Therefore, instead of testing every number from 1 to 20, generate only the
promising candidates:

2, 8, 12, 18

Test each candidate by calculating its square.

If candidate × candidate equals 144, the candidate is the required solution
and the search can stop.

For this problem:

2²  = 4    → reject
8²  = 64   → reject
12² = 144  → accept and stop
